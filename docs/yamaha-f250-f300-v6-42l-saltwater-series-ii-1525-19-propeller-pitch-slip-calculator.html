Frequently Asked Questions (Yamaha F250 / F300 V6 (4.2L) Saltwater Series II 15.25" × 19")

How do I calculate Theoretical MPH and Propeller Slip % for Yamaha F250 / F300 V6 (4.2L) Saltwater Series II 15.25" × 19"?

First divide engine WOT RPM (5,800) by the lower-unit gear ratio (1.75) to get 3,314 prop-shaft RPM. Multiply by 19" pitch and divide by 1,056 (inches per statute mile × 60 min/hr) to get 59.6 Theoretical MPH. At 54.5 GPS MPH, slip is $(1 - [54.5 \div 59.6]) \times 100 = 8.5\%$.

What happens if I change from a 19-pitch to a 21-pitch or 17-pitch prop?

Every 1 inch of pitch alters Wide-Open-Throttle RPM by 150 to 200 RPM. Dropping to a 17"-pitch prop increases WOT by ~350 RPM (to ~6,150 RPM) and boosts hole-shot acceleration for heavy loads or watersports. Stepping up to a 21"-pitch prop lowers WOT by ~350 RPM (to ~5,450 RPM).

Should I run a 3-blade (19p) or 4-blade (17p) propeller on Yamaha F250 / F300 V6 (4.2L) Saltwater Series II 15.25" × 19"?

Choose a 3-blade (15.25" × 19") stainless steel propeller for maximum top-end GPS speed on light-to-medium hulls. Choose a 4-blade (15.25" × 17") propeller if you run a heavy pontoon, offshore center console, or tow wakeboarders/tubers where hole-shot stern lift and rough-water grip matter most.
